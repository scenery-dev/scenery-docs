## What they are

The **registry** is where Scenery stores tools: every version of every skill, MCP server and plugin your organization has. The **catalog** is the part of the registry an employee can browse and install: tools approved for the organization, plus the employee's own private tools.

## Why they exist

Without a central place, tools spread by copy and paste, and nobody knows which version is running where. The registry gives every tool one source of truth. The catalog turns "approved" into something employees can actually find.

## What an admin sees

- **Skills**, **MCP Servers** and **Plugins** in the admin navigation list everything in the registry, including tools that are not yet approved.
- **Skills** shows version history and lets you [archive or revoke](/concepts/archive-vs-revoke/) a skill.
- **MCP Servers** shows each server as approved or blocked.

## What an employee sees

The portal's **Browse** pages show approved tools. Tools the employee imported for themselves are marked private. Items waiting on review are marked **Pending**. See [Find and install tools](/employees/find-and-install-tools/).
