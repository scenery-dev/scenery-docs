## What they are

- **Archive** takes a skill out of circulation. It leaves the catalog, so nobody can install it, but devices that already have it keep it. The skill and its history are kept, and you can unarchive it.
- **Revoke** is the kill switch. It stops the skill from being installed and removes it from every device that has it.

## Why both exist

Sometimes a skill is just outdated, and you want it gone from the catalog without disrupting people. Archive does that. Sometimes a tool is a problem and must stop working now. Revoke does that. Skills are never hard-deleted, so the history stays available for audit.

## What an admin sees

On a skill's page under **Skills**:

- **Archive Skill** asks for confirmation (**Yes, Archive**). **Unarchive Skill** reverses it.
- **Revoke Access** shows the impact first, then removes the skill. A revoked skill shows a **Revoked** badge in the list. You can unrevoke it later.

Users and devices have their own controls. See [Devices and pairing](/concepts/devices-and-pairing/) and [Users and organizations](/concepts/users-and-orgs/).
