If you find a security issue in Scenery, the runner or this documentation, please tell us.

## How to report

Email **security@scenery.dev** with the details: what you found, where, and the steps to reproduce it.

Please do not include real credentials or customer data in your report.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge reports within 3 business days.
- We follow coordinated disclosure. Please give us a reasonable chance to fix the issue before you publish it.
- We do not run a bug bounty program.
